## Phase 77-79 implementation boundary

Phase 77 integrates raw observation, bounded representation/state, uncertainty, experiment selection, action/observation feedback, discrepancy detection, and model revision into one bounded loop.

Phase 78 adds bounded structural transfer across environments/regimes with explicit mismatch rejection.

Phase 79 adds bounded long-horizon discrepancy-driven self-correction with a hard revision budget and fail-closed termination.

These phases are implemented but are not acceptance-certified until the regression and stronger multi-seed/held-out/adversarial gates are executed.
